Runa

A Mac app I am exploring to help people organize their thoughts, write in their own voice, and manage their time and priorities.

Status
In progress · Concept and product direction
Platform
Native Mac companion
Focus
Writing · Priorities · Intentional use of time
Data boundary
User-provided information and activity inside Runa only
CHAPTER 01

Problem

Runa started with a problem I had myself. I wanted help organizing my thoughts and writing more efficiently without losing my own voice. I am now exploring how that same idea could extend to managing my time and priorities.

CHAPTER 02

Constraints

  • No outside data collection or tracking, ever.
  • No screen monitoring, keystroke logging, browser history, or tracking of other applications.
  • Personalization is limited to information the user gives Runa and activity inside Runa.
  • Any in-app behavioral data must be transparent, controllable, and useful to the user.
  • No diagnosis or claims of treating ADHD.
CHAPTER 03

Approach

I am exploring a native Mac app that learns from writing samples and helps with outlining, editing, tasks, and reminders. I want the person using it to remain responsible for the ideas and writing. I am still working through the design, so these are planned features rather than released functionality.
