Runa

A native Mac companion in progress, designed to help people think clearly, manage competing priorities, write in their own voice, and use their time more intentionally.

Status
In progress · Concept and product direction
Platform
Native Mac companion
Focus
Writing · Priorities · Intentional use of time
Data boundary
User-provided information and activity inside Runa only
CHAPTER 01

Problem

Writing, competing priorities, and an overloaded schedule can make it difficult to think clearly. Runa began as an idea for a writing assistant that learns a person’s voice from examples and offers scaffolding while leaving the creative and intellectual work to them.

CHAPTER 02

Constraints

  • No outside data collection or tracking, ever.
  • No screen monitoring, keystroke logging, browser history, or tracking of other applications.
  • Personalization is limited to information the user gives Runa and activity inside Runa.
  • Any in-app behavioral data must be transparent, controllable, and useful to the user.
  • No diagnosis or claims of treating ADHD.
CHAPTER 03

Approach

The concept brings voice-aware writing support together with task management, reminders, workload awareness, and personalized insights. It is being shaped as a thoughtful native Mac partner that helps someone decide what matters and move forward without becoming another application that demands their attention. The project is in progress; these are design intentions rather than released features.
