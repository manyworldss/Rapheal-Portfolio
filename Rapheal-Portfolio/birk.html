CHAPTER 01

Problem

Monthly audit preparation required quality staff to reconcile more than 9,000 rows of shipping, receiving, and RMA-NCR logs. Misspelled supplier names, mixed-unit quantities, and historical spreadsheets made the work slow and difficult to check.

CHAPTER 02

Constraints

  • No in-house software developers to maintain a server or troubleshoot terminal commands.
  • Keep source files read-only and preserve existing Excel formulas, charts, and historical months.
  • Support California and Florida sites with an entirely local workflow.
  • Make exceptions visible where staff already work: inside Excel.
CHAPTER 03

Approach

Designed a guided three-step interface in plain English. A deterministic rules engine normalizes names, parses quantities, and calculates customer and supplier quality and on-time delivery metrics. Staff can review before-and-after figures, update an existing YTD workbook, or build a fresh report from a master template.

CHAPTER 04

Outcome

The repository reports processing 9,000+ rows across four reporting workbooks in under 60 seconds. The tool runs locally and records exceptions in a human-readable Outliers tab, giving staff a way to review the automation in their familiar workbook.

CHAPTER 05

Lessons Learned

Automation has to make sense to the person who inherits it. Plain-language steps, visible exceptions, and preservation of familiar spreadsheets matter as much as processing speed.
