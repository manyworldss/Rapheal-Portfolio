CHAPTER 01

Problem

A lot of the quality team’s work was manual. Monthly audits meant reviewing more than 9,000 rows of shipping, receiving, and RMA-NCR data, where supplier names were sometimes misspelled and quantities used different units. Supplier certifications also had to be tracked and renewed, which meant keeping an eye on expiration dates and following up with suppliers.

CHAPTER 02

Constraints

  • No in-house software developers to maintain a server or troubleshoot terminal commands.
  • Keep source files read-only and preserve existing Excel formulas, charts, and historical months.
  • Support California and Florida sites with an entirely local workflow.
  • Show exceptions in Excel so staff can review them.
CHAPTER 03

Approach

I work directly with the people doing the work to find the slow, manual steps, then build tools they can run without me. For audits, I built a three-step workflow that normalizes supplier names, parses quantities, and calculates customer and supplier quality and on-time delivery metrics. Staff compare the figures before updating the year-to-date workbook, and anything that looks off goes to an Excel tab for review. For supplier certifications, I built a tracker that keeps every certification in one place, tracks renewal requirements, and sends suppliers reminder emails automatically.

CHAPTER 04

Outcome

The audit tool processes more than 9,000 rows and generates audit reports in seconds for 12 staff across two sites, inside the Excel workflow they already used. Corrections stay reviewable in the workbook, so a person still signs off on every audit. The certification tracker keeps supplier records up to date without anyone chasing renewals by hand.

CHAPTER 05

Lessons Learned

One of my biggest considerations was how the team would maintain this software without a dedicated developer. It needed to be easy to run, and if something went wrong, the staff needed to understand why.
