CHAPTER 01

Problem

The quality team had to review more than 9,000 rows of shipping, receiving, and RMA-NCR data for monthly audits. Supplier names were sometimes misspelled, quantities used different units, and historical spreadsheets had to be maintained. Much of the work involved checking and correcting data manually.

CHAPTER 02

Constraints

  • No in-house software developers to maintain a server or troubleshoot terminal commands.
  • Keep source files read-only and preserve existing Excel formulas, charts, and historical months.
  • Support California and Florida sites with an entirely local workflow.
  • Show exceptions in Excel so staff can review them.
CHAPTER 03

Approach

I built a three-step workflow that normalizes supplier names, parses quantities, and calculates customer and supplier quality and on-time delivery metrics. Staff can compare the figures before updating an existing year-to-date workbook or generating a new report from a template. I also added an Excel tab for exceptions that require review.

CHAPTER 04

Outcome

The tool can process more than 9,000 rows and generate audit reports in seconds. It was built for 12 staff across two sites, using their existing Excel workflow. Staff can review corrections directly in the workbook.

CHAPTER 05

Lessons Learned

One of my biggest considerations was how the team would maintain this software without a dedicated developer. It needed to be easy to run, and if something went wrong, the staff needed to understand why.
